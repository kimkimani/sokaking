## Betting Responsibly

More coverage means more chances to bet, which makes the basics matter even more, not less:

- **Stake small, consistent amounts per match**, especially across a jackpot's 13-plus legs, where the temptation to overreach on a "sure" line is highest.
- **Treat every prediction as one informed opinion, not a promise.** Wider coverage doesn't mean higher certainty on any single pick.
- **Don't chase losses by increasing stakes.** A rough matchday doesn't get fixed by doubling down on the next one.
- **Use the deposit and loss limits** most licensed platforms — SportPesa, Betika, Odibets, Betway, and others — already provide.
- **This is strictly an 18+ activity.**

Kenya's betting industry is regulated, currently transitioning from the Betting Control and Licensing Board (BCLB) to the newly established Gambling Regulatory Authority (GRA) under the Gambling Control Act, 2025. Licensed operators are what make payouts — jackpot or otherwise — reliable.
